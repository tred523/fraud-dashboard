Add AI verdict system to the fraud dashboard.

1. Create server/verdict.js that calculates a final verdict for each visitor_id based on all available signals:

Collect all signals for a visitor:
- Max risk_score across all their events
- Any anti_detect_browser flags
- Any virtual_machine flags  
- Bot probability from behavior_events
- Number of anomalies linked to this visitor
- Payment sharing with other accounts
- Rapid IP changes
- VM/MacIntel contradictions

Calculate verdict score 0-100 and assign:
- TRUSTED (0-25): clean device, no anomalies, normal behavior
- MONITOR (26-50): some suspicious signals, worth watching
- SUSPICIOUS (51-75): multiple red flags, investigate
- BLOCK (76-100): clear fraud indicators, recommend blocking
